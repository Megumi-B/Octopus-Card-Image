### Usage
(1) [Search](https://github.com/Megumi-B/Octopus-Card-Image/tree/main?search=1) the card image by name or series.<br>
(2) Download it to your PC or iPhone.<br>
(3) Connect to AirCard.<br>
(4) Upload and replace the current card skin.<br>
